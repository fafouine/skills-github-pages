# beekeeping 101
Beekeepers must wear suitable protective gear, keep tools clean, and inspect hives regularly for signs of disease, pests, or stress. They must provide colonies with enough food and clean water, and handle frames gently to avoid harming bees or the queen.

Beekeepers must not open hives unnecessarily, use treatments carelessly, or move colonies without checking local rules. They should avoid disturbing bees in cold, windy, or rainy weather, and never leave exposed honey or syrup where it could attract pests or spread disease.

Responsible beekeeping also means learning local regulations, keeping accurate records, and asking experienced beekeepers or veterinarians for help when a colony appears unwell.
